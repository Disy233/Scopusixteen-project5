Reviewer Feedback

Comments requiring author attention

2 Reports
Reviewer 1 Major Revision

The manuscript addresses a relevant area of digital education. The methodology requires additional explanation, particularly regarding the criteria used to identify and organise the reviewed literature.

The discussion should also provide a stronger connection between the identified challenges and the proposed implications for educational institutions.

Reviewer 2 Major Revision

The manuscript has potential, but the contribution should be more clearly distinguished from existing research. Additional explanation of limitations would strengthen the paper.

Please revise the conclusion so that the claims are directly supported by the evidence discussed in the manuscript.

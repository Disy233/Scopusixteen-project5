PUBLISHING MODEL

Hybrid Publishing

A publishing model that can combine subscription-based access with an optional open-access route for eligible articles.

Prototype publishing information

This page describes a proposed publishing model for the Scopusixteen website prototype. Individual journals may adopt different publishing models, fees, licences and access policies once formally established.

What Is Hybrid Publishing?

Hybrid publishing generally refers to a journal model in which a journal operates primarily through a subscription or other access model while providing an option for individual articles to be made openly accessible when the applicable conditions are met.

Under such a model, authors may have an open-access option for an eligible article, potentially subject to an Article Processing Charge (APC) and the journal's applicable licensing and copyright arrangements.

The exact availability of hybrid publishing should always be confirmed on the individual journal's official information page.

Understanding the Publishing Models

H

Hybrid

A journal may combine subscription access with an optional open-access route for selected articles.

O

Open Access

Articles are made openly accessible according to the journal's open-access policy and applicable licence.

S

Subscription

Access is provided through subscriptions, institutional arrangements or other permitted access routes.

How the Hybrid Route Can Work

A typical workflow can involve the following stages. The exact process may vary by journal.

1
Select a journal Review the journal's aims, scope, publishing model and author requirements.
2
Submit your manuscript Submit the manuscript through the applicable submission process.
3
Editorial and peer review The manuscript undergoes the applicable editorial assessment and review process.
4
Editorial decision The journal makes an editorial decision according to its policy.
5
Select an available access route Where the journal provides a hybrid option, eligible authors may choose the applicable access route.
6
Production and publication The accepted article proceeds through the applicable production and publication process.

Potential Benefits

Flexible Access

Hybrid models can provide different access routes depending on the journal and article.

Wider Visibility

Where an article is legitimately published open access, it may be accessible to readers without a subscription.

Institutional Access

Subscription arrangements may continue to support institutional access to journal content.

Author Choice

Where permitted, authors may have a choice between available access routes.

Article Processing Charges

An APC may apply when an author chooses an applicable open-access route in a hybrid journal.

The amount, currency, eligibility, payment timing, waiver arrangements and included services should be stated clearly in the journal's official APC policy.

For information about the proposed APC structure on this prototype website, visit the APC information page.

Peer Review Remains Independent

Selecting or paying for an open-access option should not determine whether a manuscript is accepted.

Editorial decisions should be based on the journal's scope, editorial criteria, research quality and applicable peer-review process.

Authors should therefore be cautious of any service that promises acceptance in exchange for payment.

Copyright and Licensing

The copyright and licensing terms for an article depend on the journal's formal publishing agreement and the access route selected.

If an article is published under an open-access licence, the applicable licence should be clearly identified before publication.

Authors should check

  • Who retains copyright.
  • Which licence applies.
  • How readers may reuse the article.
  • Whether attribution is required.
  • Whether commercial reuse is permitted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does hybrid publishing mean every article is open access?

No. A hybrid journal may provide an open-access option while continuing to operate with a subscription-based access model for other content.

Is an APC always required?

Not necessarily. An APC may apply when an author selects an applicable open-access route, but the exact policy depends on the journal.

Does paying an APC guarantee acceptance?

No. Payment should never be presented as a guarantee of editorial acceptance or favourable peer review.

Can I publish through the subscription route?

Where a journal offers a subscription route, authors may follow the access and publishing conditions specified by that journal.

Are all Scopusixteen journals hybrid?

No. Each journal should have its own formally established publishing model and access policy. The journal page should be consulted for the applicable information.

Where can I find the APC information?

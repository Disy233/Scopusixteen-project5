About Scopusixteen

Scopusixteen Publishing is a modern academic publishing platform that supports both subscription and open access (APC) models.

Our mission is to advance research, connect knowledge, and make scholarly communication more transparent and efficient through responsible AI-assisted editorial tools.

Publishing modes

  • APC / Open Access – authors pay a fee; articles are free to read worldwide.
  • Subscription – institutions or individuals subscribe; selected content is paywalled.
  • Hybrid – journals offer both routes on an article-by-article basis.

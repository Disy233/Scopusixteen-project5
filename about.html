About Scopusixteen

A modern academic publishing platform built for researchers, editors and institutions.

Our Mission

Scopusixteen exists to make high-quality academic publishing more accessible, transparent and efficient. We combine rigorous peer review with modern technology and responsible AI tools to support authors and editors worldwide.

Publishing Models

APC (Open Access)

Authors pay an Article Processing Charge. The final article is freely available to everyone.

Subscription

Institutions and individuals subscribe to access the full content of the journal.

Hybrid

A mix of subscription access and optional open-access publication for individual articles.

Responsible AI

We use AI to assist with manuscript completeness checks, language improvement suggestions, metadata generation, journal-scope matching and reviewer recommendations. All AI outputs are advisory only. Final editorial decisions are always made by qualified human editors.

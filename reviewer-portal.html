Reviewer Responsibilities

Important principles for responsible peer review.

Full Policy
⚖

Confidentiality

Manuscripts and review materials should be treated as confidential and should not be shared with unauthorised individuals.

✓

Objective Assessment

Reviews should focus on scholarly quality, methodology, originality, clarity and relevance rather than personal characteristics.

!

Conflict of Interest

Reviewers should disclose potential conflicts and decline assignments where impartiality could reasonably be compromised.

◆

Constructive Feedback

Comments should help authors understand strengths, weaknesses and opportunities for improvement.

✦

Responsible AI Use

Any permitted AI-assisted review activity should respect confidentiality and applicable journal policies.

⏱

Timely Review

Reviewers should respond to invitations and complete accepted reviews within the agreed timeframe.

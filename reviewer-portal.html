Reviewer Responsibilities

Review the manuscript objectively, maintain confidentiality, identify relevant methodological or presentation concerns, disclose potential conflicts of interest and provide constructive recommendations to the editor.

Confidentiality: Manuscripts and reviewer reports should be treated as confidential editorial material. Do not share or distribute unpublished manuscript content outside the authorised review process.

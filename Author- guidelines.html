Author Guidelines

Information to help authors prepare, format and submit manuscripts for consideration by Scopusixteen Publishing journals.

Important: Individual journals may have additional requirements. Authors should always review the specific journal's aims, scope and submission instructions before submitting.

Before You Submit

Authors should ensure that their manuscript is original, appropriately prepared and suitable for the selected journal.

  • The manuscript is original and has not been published elsewhere.
  • The manuscript is not simultaneously under consideration by another journal.
  • All listed authors have approved the submitted version.
  • All authors meet appropriate authorship requirements.
  • Sources and references have been accurately cited.
  • Required ethical approvals have been obtained where applicable.
  • Conflicts of interest have been disclosed.

Article Types

Journals may accept different categories of scholarly work. Authors should select the article type that best represents their submission.

Research Article Original research presenting methods, results and discussion.
Review Article Critical synthesis and evaluation of existing research.
Systematic Review Structured review using a defined methodology.
Case Study Detailed examination of a particular case, organisation or context.
Short Communication Concise presentation of important findings or observations.
Conceptual Article Development or examination of theoretical concepts and frameworks.

Manuscript Structure

Unless the target journal specifies otherwise, manuscripts should generally contain the following elements.

  1. Title
  2. Author names and affiliations
  3. Abstract
  4. Keywords
  5. Introduction
  6. Literature review or theoretical framework, where applicable
  7. Methodology
  8. Results or findings
  9. Discussion
  10. Conclusion
  11. Declarations
  12. References

Title

The title should be concise, informative and accurately describe the main subject of the manuscript.

Avoid unnecessary abbreviations, excessive punctuation and promotional language.

Abstract

The abstract should provide a clear summary of the research and allow readers to understand the purpose and main contribution of the work.

A structured abstract may include:

  • Background
  • Objective
  • Methods
  • Results
  • Conclusion

The exact word limit may vary by journal.

Keywords

Authors should provide relevant keywords that accurately describe the manuscript. Keywords help readers discover research through search systems.

A typical submission may include approximately 4–8 keywords unless the target journal specifies otherwise.

Authors and Affiliations

All authors should have made a meaningful contribution to the research and should approve the final manuscript.

Author information should normally include:

  • Full name
  • Institutional affiliation
  • Department or faculty where applicable
  • Country
  • Corresponding author's email address

Changes to authorship after submission may require approval from all affected authors and the editorial office.

References and Citations

Authors are responsible for ensuring that references are accurate, complete and consistently formatted.

  • Cite sources that directly support the statements made in the manuscript.
  • Check author names, publication years, titles and page information.
  • Include DOI information when available and appropriate.
  • Do not fabricate references or citations.

The required citation style may vary between journals.

Figures and Tables

Figures and tables should be clearly numbered and referred to in the manuscript text.

  • Provide descriptive titles or captions.
  • Ensure figures are sufficiently clear.
  • Identify the source of reproduced material.
  • Obtain permission where copyrighted material requires permission.

Use of Artificial Intelligence

Authors may use appropriate artificial intelligence tools for limited assistance during manuscript preparation, subject to the requirements of the target journal.

Authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy, originality, integrity and intellectual content of their manuscript.

Authors should not:

  • Submit fabricated information generated by an AI system.
  • Use AI to create fake references, citations or research results.
  • Present AI systems as authors.
  • Upload confidential or sensitive manuscript information to external systems without appropriate safeguards.

Where required by the journal, authors should disclose material use of AI-assisted tools during manuscript preparation.

Research Ethics

Research involving human participants, animals, personal data or other regulated activities should comply with applicable ethical standards and institutional requirements.

Where applicable, authors should provide information about ethics approval, informed consent and relevant permissions.

Required Declarations

Depending on the article and journal, authors may be required to provide:

  • Funding information
  • Conflict of interest statement
  • Ethics approval
  • Informed consent
  • Data availability statement
  • Author contribution statement

Submission Checklist

  • Correct journal selected
  • Manuscript file prepared
  • Title and abstract completed
  • Keywords added
  • All authors listed correctly
  • References checked
  • Figures and tables checked
  • Declarations completed
  • Ethical requirements addressed
  • Manuscript checked for originality

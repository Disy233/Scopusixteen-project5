Article Processing Charges

Information about article processing charges, payment procedures, waivers and publishing models for authors.

What is an Article Processing Charge?

An Article Processing Charge (APC) is a fee that may be charged to support the publication of an accepted scholarly article, particularly under certain open-access publishing models.

Depending on the publisher and journal, publication charges may contribute toward services such as editorial administration, production, typesetting, hosting and long-term access.

An APC should never be represented as a payment that guarantees acceptance. Editorial and peer-review decisions should remain independent of payment.

What May an APC Cover?

  • Editorial administration and manuscript processing.
  • Peer-review administration where applicable.
  • Article preparation and production.
  • Typesetting and formatting.
  • Online publication and hosting.
  • Preservation and platform maintenance, where applicable.

The actual services included in an APC should be clearly specified in each journal's official fee policy.

What an APC Does Not Guarantee

Payment of an APC should not guarantee manuscript acceptance, favourable peer review, indexing, citation performance or publication in a particular issue.

Manuscripts should undergo the applicable editorial assessment and peer-review process before an acceptance decision is made.

Payment Process

A production publishing system should issue an official invoice or payment request only when a legitimate charge becomes due under the applicable journal policy.

1
Editorial decision The manuscript completes the applicable editorial process.
2
APC assessment The applicable journal fee and publishing model are confirmed.
3
Invoice A formal invoice should identify the service, amount and payment instructions.
4
Payment Payment should be processed through an approved payment channel.
5
Production Where applicable, the accepted manuscript proceeds to production and publication.

APC Waivers and Discounts

A publisher may establish waiver or discount programmes for eligible authors, institutions or geographical regions.

Any waiver programme should have transparent eligibility criteria and should not influence the editorial decision on a manuscript.

Possible waiver categories

  • Full APC waiver.
  • Partial APC discount.
  • Institutional publishing agreements.
  • Special journal programmes.

Scopusixteen's actual waiver programme should be published only after formal approval.

Refunds and Cancellations

Refund eligibility should depend on the circumstances of the payment and the publisher's formally adopted refund policy.

A production system should clearly state whether refunds are available before production, after production has started, or after publication.

Authors should retain their invoice and payment confirmation for any payment-related enquiry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does paying an APC guarantee acceptance?

No. An APC should not guarantee acceptance. Editorial and peer-review decisions should be independent of payment.

When should an APC normally be requested?

This depends on the journal's policy. The applicable timing and conditions should be clearly stated before authors make payment.

Can authors request a waiver?

If a formal waiver programme exists, eligible authors may apply according to its published criteria.

Are the prices on this page official?

No. The amounts currently shown are demonstration values for the website prototype.

Does an APC include indexing?

No publication fee should be presented as a guarantee of indexing in any database. Indexing status depends on the requirements and decisions of the relevant indexing service.

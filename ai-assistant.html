Responsible Use of AI

AI can support researchers, but it should not replace scholarly judgement or institutional requirements.

  • Verify important factual and scientific claims using reliable scholarly sources.
  • Do not submit AI-generated material without reviewing, editing and taking responsibility for its content.
  • Follow the AI disclosure requirements of the target journal or publisher.
  • Never fabricate references, data, results or citations.
  • Protect confidential manuscript, reviewer and research information when using AI systems.

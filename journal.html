About the Journal

The Journal of Digital Education provides a scholarly platform for research examining the use, development and impact of digital technologies in education.

The journal welcomes original research, systematic reviews, conceptual studies, case studies and other scholarly contributions that advance understanding of technology-enabled education.

Aims & Scope

The journal aims to encourage rigorous research into contemporary digital education and emerging approaches to teaching, learning and educational management.

Topics of interest include:

  • Artificial intelligence in education
  • Digital learning and e-learning
  • Learning management systems
  • Educational technology
  • Online and blended learning
  • Digital assessment and evaluation
  • Learning analytics
  • Virtual and augmented reality in education
  • Teacher digital competencies
  • Technology-enhanced higher education
  • Educational innovation and policy
  • Digital inclusion and accessibility

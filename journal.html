About the Journal

The Journal of Digital Education is designed as a scholarly publication platform for research examining how digital technologies influence teaching, learning, assessment and educational institutions.

The journal welcomes original research, systematic reviews, conceptual studies and evidence-based perspectives addressing developments in digital education.

The journal's actual editorial policies, publication schedule, indexing status, ISSN and editorial appointments must be confirmed by the publisher before being represented as official.

Aims & Scope

The journal aims to provide a platform for rigorous academic discussion concerning the development, implementation and evaluation of digital approaches to education.

Areas of interest may include, but are not limited to:

Digital learning and online education
Artificial intelligence in education
Learning management systems
Educational data analytics
Virtual and augmented learning
Digital assessment
Computer-supported collaborative learning
Digital literacy and skills
Technology-enhanced teaching
Educational innovation and policy

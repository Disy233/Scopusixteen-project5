Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a manuscript?

Use the Submit Manuscript option in the Author Portal and follow the journal's submission requirements.

Does submitting guarantee publication?

No. Submission does not guarantee acceptance or publication. Manuscripts may be subject to editorial screening and peer review.

Can I ask about an existing manuscript?

Yes. Include the manuscript reference number in your enquiry where one has been issued.

Is the payment system currently live?

Payment interfaces displayed on the current prototype should not be interpreted as proof of live payment processing.

Ready to Submit?

Review the author requirements and submit your manuscript through the Author Portal.

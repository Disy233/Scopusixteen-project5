Abstract

Artificial intelligence is increasingly influencing educational practices, curriculum development and digital learning environments. This article examines the potential role of artificial intelligence tools in computer education, with particular attention to teaching effectiveness, personalised learning, student engagement and assessment.

The study considers opportunities and challenges associated with incorporating AI-supported tools into computer education and discusses implications for educators, students and educational institutions.

1. Introduction

Digital technologies have become an increasingly important component of contemporary education. Universities, colleges and schools are adopting digital platforms to improve access to learning resources and facilitate communication between educators and students.

The emergence of artificial intelligence has expanded these possibilities by introducing systems capable of supporting content generation, personalised learning, automated feedback and educational analytics.

Despite these opportunities, the adoption of AI in education also raises questions relating to academic integrity, data protection, transparency, bias and the changing role of educators.

2. Discussion

AI-supported educational technologies can provide students with additional opportunities for personalised learning. Adaptive systems may help learners access resources according to their individual needs and progress.

Educators may also use AI tools to support lesson planning, formative assessment and the identification of areas where students require additional assistance.

2.1 Opportunities

  • Personalised learning experiences
  • Automated formative feedback
  • Improved access to educational resources
  • Support for educators
  • Enhanced learning analytics

2.2 Challenges

  • Academic integrity
  • Data privacy and security
  • Algorithmic bias
  • Digital inequality
  • Dependence on automated systems

3. Conclusion

Artificial intelligence has the potential to contribute significantly to the development of computer education. However, effective adoption requires appropriate institutional policies, educator training and responsible use of technology.

Future research should examine how AI-supported educational tools influence learning outcomes, student behaviour, academic integrity and teaching practices across different educational contexts.

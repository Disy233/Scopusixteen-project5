Abstract

Artificial intelligence is increasingly influencing teaching, learning, assessment and educational administration. This demonstration abstract presents a model for how a scholarly article can examine the opportunities and challenges associated with AI-enabled digital education.

The article interface is designed to support clear presentation of research findings while providing readers with structured access to metadata, references, declarations and related information.

Keywords

Artificial Intelligence Digital Education Educational Technology Online Learning Learning Analytics

1. Introduction

Digital technologies have transformed many aspects of education. The expansion of online learning platforms, digital resources and learning analytics has created new opportunities for institutions and educators.

Artificial intelligence represents another significant development because computational systems can assist with tasks such as content recommendation, personalised learning, assessment and administrative support.

However, responsible adoption requires consideration of accuracy, transparency, privacy, bias, academic integrity and the role of human judgement.

4. Discussion

AI-assisted educational systems may provide benefits when implemented with appropriate pedagogical, technical and governance frameworks.

Nevertheless, technology should complement rather than automatically replace professional educational judgement. Institutions should establish appropriate safeguards for data protection, transparency and responsible technology use.

5. Conclusion

Artificial intelligence presents significant research opportunities within digital education. Future studies can examine effectiveness, equity, reliability, responsible implementation and long-term educational outcomes.
